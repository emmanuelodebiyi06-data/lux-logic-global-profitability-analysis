# Lux-Logic Global: Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

How can a business generate millions in sales and still retain only a small portion as profit?

This project analyzes the sales and profitability performance of Lux-Logic Global across cities, countries, regions, and time periods from 2017 to 2020.

The analysis focuses on identifying where sales are being generated, which locations contribute most to profitability, where losses are concentrated, and how sales performance changes over time.

The goal is to transform sales data into insights that can support better decisions around market performance, cost management, inventory planning, and resource allocation.

---

## Business Problem

Revenue alone does not provide a complete picture of business performance.

This analysis addresses key questions such as:

- How much sales revenue is being generated?
- How much of that revenue is retained as gross profit?
- Which cities contribute most to profitability?
- Which cities are generating the largest losses?
- How does profitability vary across countries?
- What patterns can be observed in sales over time?
- Which markets require closer performance and cost review?

## Key Insights

### Profitability by City

The dashboard identifies the cities contributing most strongly to gross profit, including Seattle, Toronto, Edmonton, Loveland, Phoenix, Toledo, Casper, Austin, Saint Louis, and Orlando.

This shows that profitability is concentrated in specific locations rather than being evenly distributed across the business.

### Loss-Making Cities

The analysis highlights locations with significant gross profit losses, including Sandy City, Vancouver, Colomiers, Chandler, Bellingham, Courbevoie, Fernley, Lacey, Odessa, and Bad Soden.

These locations provide areas for further investigation into factors such as pricing, cost structure, product mix, and sales volume.

### Sales Trend

The sales trend shows fluctuations across the period, with recurring peaks around the third quarter.

Understanding these patterns can support inventory planning, marketing timing, and sales resource allocation.

### Country Performance

Profit and loss rates vary considerably across the countries included in the dashboard.

Canada shows the highest profit rate among the countries displayed, while Australia records a substantially higher loss rate.

This highlights the importance of evaluating performance at country and market level rather than relying only on overall company figures.

### Regional Performance

The geographic view provides a high-level perspective of sales distribution across major regions and complements the city- and country-level analysis.

## Business Implications

The analysis can support management in:

- Reviewing the cost structure of consistently loss-making locations.
- Monitoring pricing and product costs before sales decisions.
- Identifying profitable markets for closer attention.
- Planning inventory and marketing activities around recurring demand periods.
- Regularly reviewing sales and profitability performance.
- Comparing market-level performance to identify areas requiring further investigation.
